# Attendance Skill

## Purpose

Guide attendance, shift, roster, leave, permission, WFH, overtime, and timesheet work.

## Guidelines

- Document timezones, grace periods, shift rules, holidays, approvals, and corrections.

## Do

- Preserve raw clock events.
- Separate raw attendance from calculated attendance results.

## Don't

- Overwrite source clock data.
- Ignore cross-day shifts or holidays.

## Examples

- Night shift clock-out after midnight must still map to the correct roster date.

## Checklist

- [ ] Timezone defined
- [ ] Shift edge cases documented
- [ ] Approval flow mapped
- [ ] Audit events defined
