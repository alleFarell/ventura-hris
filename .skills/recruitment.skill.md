# Recruitment Skill

## Purpose

Guide recruitment, candidate, interview, and hiring features.

## Guidelines

- Separate candidate data from employee master data until hiring is complete.
- Document consent, retention, and screening rules.

## Do

- Track vacancy stages and interview outcomes.
- Audit hiring decisions and status changes.

## Don't

- Convert candidates to employees without documented workflow.
- Use AI screening without explainability and human review.

## Examples

- Candidate status changes from interview to offer must be timestamped and attributable.

## Checklist

- [ ] Workflow stages defined
- [ ] Consent and retention reviewed
- [ ] Hiring handoff documented
- [ ] AI use reviewed
