# Performance Skill

## Purpose

Guide KPI, goal, appraisal, and feedback features.

## Guidelines

- Treat performance data as sensitive and permissioned.
- Document cycles, scoring models, calibration, and visibility rules.

## Do

- Version appraisal templates and scoring scales.
- Audit reviewer changes and finalization.

## Don't

- Mix draft feedback with final appraisal records.
- Let AI generate final evaluations without human accountability.

## Examples

- KPI weights must sum to documented limits before appraisal submission.

## Checklist

- [ ] Cycle defined
- [ ] Visibility mapped
- [ ] Scoring documented
- [ ] Audit events defined
